# KELT-25 Results Summary

## Numerical Results

The fitted power-2 limb darkening parameters were:

\[
h_1 = 0.747 \pm [\text{0.021}]
\]

\[
h_2 = 0.484 \pm [\text{0.162}]
\]

These values are taken from the final fit report generated by the approximately
4000 step MCMC run.

## Overview

The $h_2$ parameter initially takes longer to settle, however converges at around 2500 steps though not as neatly as other parameters including $h_1$. The other parameters also converge at the 2500 step mark, though remain a lot more consistent.

Irregular shaped contours on the corner plot indicate that the posterior distributions are not simple Gaussian uncertainties, but rather that the parameters have strong interdependencies between one another.
